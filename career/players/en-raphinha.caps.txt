From the streets of Porto Alegre to the captain's armband at Barcelona. This is the career of Raphinha.
He was born in Porto Alegre, Brazil, in 1996, and at 19 he moved to Portugal, to Vitória de Guimarães.
Then came Sporting, where he won the Portuguese Cup and the League Cup, before a move to Rennes in France.
In 2020, he joined Leeds United, and scored 17 goals in 67 games.
In the summer of 2022, he moved to Barcelona.
And in his first season, he won La Liga.
Then came his best season. 34 goals, and a domestic Treble. La Liga, the Copa del Rey, and the Super Cup.
He was the Champions League's joint top scorer with 13 goals, and La Liga's Player of the Season.
Last season, he won a third league title with Barcelona.
This season, he became Barcelona's captain. The first Brazilian ever to hold the role.
And he started with 14 goals in his first eight games, a club record.
For Barcelona so far, 94 goals in 191 games, and a new contract until 2030.
For Brazil, two World Cups, and 12 goals in 43 games.
