From a small town in Norway to the top of world football. This is the career of Erling Haaland.
He was born in Leeds in the year 2000, while his father played there, and he grew up in Bryne, in Norway, where it all began.
Then came Molde, and from there, Red Bull Salzburg.
At Salzburg, he scored 29 goals in 27 games.
In January 2020, he joined Borussia Dortmund. 86 goals in 89 games, and the German Cup.
In the summer of 2022, he moved to Manchester City.
In his first season, he scored 36 Premier League goals. A record for a single season.
And in that same season, he won the Treble. The Premier League, the FA Cup, and the Champions League.
Another Premier League title followed. Then last season, the FA Cup, the League Cup, and his third Golden Boot.
For City so far, 176 goals in 211 games.
With Norway, he led his country to its first World Cup since 1998, and he scored 7 goals there.
His two goals knocked out Brazil in the round of 16, and Norway reached the quarter-finals for the first time ever.
And he is Norway's all-time top scorer. 66 goals in 60 games.
