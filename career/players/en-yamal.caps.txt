From Rocafonda to the top of the world. This is the career of Lamine Yamal.
He was born in July 2007, and grew up in Rocafonda, in Mataró. At six, Barcelona spotted him, and he joined La Masia.
In April 2023, he made his first-team debut at just 15, and won La Liga that same season.
Months later, at 16, he became the youngest player ever to play for Spain.
In the summer of 2024, he won the Euros with Spain. The youngest scorer in the tournament's history, and its Young Player.
Then came Barcelona's domestic Treble. La Liga, the Copa del Rey, and the Super Cup.
He finished second in the Ballon d'Or, and took the number 10 shirt.
Last season, another league title, and La Liga's Player of the Season.
In the summer of 2026, he won the World Cup with Spain, beating Argentina 1-0 in the final.
He became the youngest player ever to win both the World Cup and the Euros.
He is the first player to win the Kopa Trophy three times, and the top-scoring teenager in Champions League history.
For Barcelona so far, 62 goals in 167 games, and a contract until 2031.
