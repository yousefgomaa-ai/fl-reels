From a small town in Norway to the top of world football. This is the career of Erling Haaland.
He was born in Leeds in the year two thousand, while his father played there, and he grew up in Bryne, in Norway, where it all began.
Then came Molde, and from there, Red Bull Salzburg.
At Salzburg, he scored twenty-nine goals in twenty-seven games.
In January twenty twenty, he joined Borussia Dortmund. Eighty-six goals in eighty-nine games, and the German Cup.
In the summer of twenty twenty-two, he moved to Manchester City.
In his first season, he scored thirty-six Premier League goals. A record for a single season.
And in that same season, he won the Treble. The Premier League, the FA Cup, and the Champions League.
Another Premier League title followed. Then last season, the FA Cup, the League Cup, and his third Golden Boot.
For City so far, one hundred and seventy-six goals in two hundred and eleven games.
With Norway, he led his country to its first World Cup since nineteen ninety-eight, and he scored seven goals there.
His two goals knocked out Brazil in the round of sixteen, and Norway reached the quarter-finals for the first time ever.
And he is Norway's all-time top scorer. Sixty-six goals in sixty games.
