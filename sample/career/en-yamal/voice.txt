From Rocafonda to the top of the world. This is the career of Lamine Yamal.
He was born in July two thousand and seven, and grew up in Rocafonda, in Mataró. At six, Barcelona spotted him, and he joined La Masia.
In April twenty twenty-three, he made his first-team debut at just fifteen, and won La Liga that same season.
Months later, at sixteen, he became the youngest player ever to play for Spain.
In the summer of twenty twenty-four, he won the Euros with Spain. The youngest scorer in the tournament's history, and its Young Player.
Then came Barcelona's domestic Treble. La Liga, the Copa del Rey, and the Super Cup.
He finished second in the Ballon d'Or, and took the number ten shirt.
Last season, another league title, and La Liga's Player of the Season.
In the summer of twenty twenty-six, he won the World Cup with Spain, beating Argentina one nil in the final.
He became the youngest player ever to win both the World Cup and the Euros.
He is the first player to win the Kopa Trophy three times, and the top-scoring teenager in Champions League history.
For Barcelona so far, sixty-two goals in one hundred and sixty-seven games, and a contract until twenty thirty-one.
