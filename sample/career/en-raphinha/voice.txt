From the streets of Porto Alegre to the captain's armband at Barcelona. This is the career of Raphinha.
He was born in Porto Alegre, Brazil, in nineteen ninety-six, and at nineteen he moved to Portugal, to Vitória de Guimarães.
Then came Sporting, where he won the Portuguese Cup and the League Cup, before a move to Rennes in France.
In twenty twenty, he joined Leeds United, and scored seventeen goals in sixty-seven games.
In the summer of twenty twenty-two, he moved to Barcelona.
And in his first season, he won La Liga.
Then came his best season. Thirty-four goals, and a domestic Treble. La Liga, the Copa del Rey, and the Super Cup.
He was the Champions League's joint top scorer with thirteen goals, and La Liga's Player of the Season.
Last season, he won a third league title with Barcelona.
This season, he became Barcelona's captain. The first Brazilian ever to hold the role.
And he started with fourteen goals in his first eight games, a club record.
For Barcelona so far, ninety-four goals in one hundred and ninety-one games, and a new contract until twenty thirty.
For Brazil, two World Cups, and twelve goals in forty-three games.
