# Two Paths: idea queue

**Read `stick/WORKFLOW.md` first: the owner's standing rules.**

Wait for "نفذ" before making the next reel. Make them in this order, one at a time, and send each one when it's done.


## The first 1.5 seconds (most important rule)

Every reel opens with a strong hook in its first 1.5 s. Check it on a still of frame 0 and of 1.5 s before rendering.
- Frame 0 already shows the conflict or the strangest image of the reel (the monster shadow, the 25% button,
  the giant pile) - never two people just standing.
- Something moves from frame 0 (no slow fade-ins, no empty room).
- The contrast between the two panels is visible without reading.
- At most one short line on screen in that time (a label or one bubble), readable in under a second.
- Sound from frame 0: a hit, a sting or the first note - no silent start.
- Length follows the idea: symbolic 10-14 s, simple situations 12-15 s, stories 18-25 s (not a fixed 18 s).

## Lessons from viewers

- "Great idea, wrong execution" (Black Friday, Nov 2026). From now on:
  1. The twist is the biggest thing on screen: large, centred, on screen for at least 1.5 s, and it lands by 60-70%
     of the reel - never a small notification at the end.
  2. One counter at most per panel, and no extra cards or signs that don't serve the joke. If it isn't needed to
     understand the story, cut it.
  3. Check the logic against real life before building it (would an American viewer argue with it?). If the
     comparison can be argued, change it.
  4. Bigger figures and props; everything must read on a phone at a glance.

## Rules for every reel

- Split screen. Both people start in **exactly the same situation**. The idea has to be clear from the first scene.
- Calm and slow. The music starts soft and builds until the twist. A warm chord under the closing line, which is
  **two short lines that answer each other**, then the FOLLOW FOR MORE card. 18 seconds, and it loops.
- **Western audience** (US / Europe): situations from American and European daily life that work in both.
  Clock in **12-hour AM/PM**, money in **$**, and **no temperatures on screen** (Europe uses °C, the US °F).
- **Alternate the openings, A then B**, until we review the numbers:
  - A: current style, dark room, calm start.
  - B: brighter room, a close-up that pulls back, and the speech bubble plus action on screen from frame 0.
  - Order so far: #10 A (fridge), #11 B (jacket), Black Friday A, Check the price B, $7 latte A, Tipping B. **Next one is A.**
- **Different music for every reel** (`stick/music.py`). Don't use the same track twice in a row:
  piano_c, music_box_d, guitar_g, marimba_f, lofi_eb, strings_am, kalimba_e, harp_bb.
  Used so far: #10 music_box_d, #11 marimba_f, Black Friday guitar_g, Check the price kalimba_e, $7 latte lofi_eb, Tipping harp_bb.
- One version per reel (no more two copies), and send the title with every video.
