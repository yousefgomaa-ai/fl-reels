# Two Paths: 30 short ideas (owner, 2026-11-19)

Everyday moments everyone lives, two ways of handling them, a twist at the end and one line people remember.
Our own ideas, nothing taken from other channels. Make them one by one, in the channel's current style, in this order.

| # | Idea | Twist at the end | Last line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"I'm 5 minutes away"**: the friend texts it from bed; you wait at the café.
