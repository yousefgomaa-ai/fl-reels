# Two Paths: symbolic reels (separate list)

Our own ideas in the minimal symbolic style that the reference channel (Simply Animated) shows works: black background,
solid white figures, one visual metaphor readable in the first frame without reading, two short concept labels
(no clocks, counters or speech bubbles), one accent light, the title is a short saying. Ideas and metaphors are
ours; none of them copy a reference reel.


## The first 1.5 seconds (most important rule)

Every reel opens with a strong hook in its first 1.5 s. Check it on a still of frame 0 and of 1.5 s before rendering.
- Frame 0 already shows the conflict or the strangest image of the reel (the monster shadow, the 25% button,
  the giant pile) - never two people just standing.
- Something moves from frame 0 (no slow fade-ins, no empty room).
- The contrast between the two panels is visible without reading.
- At most one short line on screen in that time (a label or one bubble), readable in under a second.
- Sound from frame 0: a hit, a sting or the first note - no silent start.
- Length follows the idea: symbolic 10-14 s, simple situations 12-15 s, stories 18-25 s (not a fixed 18 s).

| 4 | KNOCKS / SEARCHES | A big locked door. One knocks and waits for someone to open it. The other checks his own pockets and finds a key labelled WORK. | The door opens from the inside, for the one who had the key. | The key was in your pocket all along. |
| 5 | EARNS MORE / KEEPS MORE | Two buckets under two taps. The big tap fills a bucket full of holes (labelled SPENDING); the small tap fills a bucket with none. | The small tap's bucket is full; the big one is still empty. | It's not what you earn. It's what you keep. |

| 8 | SCROLLS / BUILDS | Same hour every day. One scrolls an endless feed; the other lays one brick. A day counter runs to 365. | A sore thumb vs a finished house. | Same hour. One built a house. |

| 10 | SAFE / STORM | Two seeds. One is planted under a glass dome, safe from wind and rain. The other is planted in the open, in storms. | The safe one is still a small sprout; the open one is a big tree with deep roots. | Storms grow roots. |
